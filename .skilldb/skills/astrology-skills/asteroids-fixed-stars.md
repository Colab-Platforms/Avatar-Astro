# asteroids-fixed-stars

Calculate and interpret asteroids, fixed stars, Arabic parts/lots, and Sabian symbols in a natal or transit chart. Covers the major asteroids (Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta, Chiron), named asteroids (Eros, Psyche, Lilith variants), fixed stars (Regulus, Algol, Spica, Sirius, Fomalhaut, Antares, Aldebaran, etc.), Arabic parts (Part of Fortune, Part of Spirit, Part of Eros, Part of Marriage), and Sabian symbols for each degree. Use when the user wants asteroid placements, fixed star conjunctions, lot calculations, or Sabian symbol lookups.
