# lunar-calendar

Moon phase calendar, void-of-course Moon tables, eclipses in natal chart houses, lunar gardening calendar, and lunar cycle planning. Covers the 8 lunar phases (New, Crescent, First Quarter, Gibbous, Full, Disseminating, Last Quarter, Balsamic), void-of-course Moon periods and their meaning, Moon sign transits (2.5 days each), eclipse interpretation in natal houses, lunar gardening (planting/harvesting by Moon phase and sign), and practical Moon-based scheduling. Use when the user wants Moon phase information, VOC Moon times, eclipse house analysis, or lunar-based planning.
