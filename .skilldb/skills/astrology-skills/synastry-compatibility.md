# synastry-compatibility

Analyze relationship compatibility using synastry (chart overlay), composite charts (midpoint method), Davison relationship charts, draconic synastry, and progressed synastry. Covers inter-aspects, house overlays, double-whammies, Venus/Mars dynamics, Moon compatibility, Saturn bonds, Pluto intensity, nodal connections, and overall compatibility scoring. Use when comparing two people's charts for romantic, business, friendship, or family compatibility.
