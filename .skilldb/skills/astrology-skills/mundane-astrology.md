# mundane-astrology

Mundane astrology — the astrology of nations, world events, political cycles, and collective destiny. Covers ingress charts (Aries ingress for the year), great conjunctions (Jupiter-Saturn and outer planet cycles), eclipse impact on nations, national horoscopes (founding charts of countries), planetary cycles and historical eras, mundane house meanings, lunation charts for monthly forecasting, cardinal ingress interpretation, and geopolitical astrology. Use when analyzing world events, political trends, economic cycles, or the astrological signature of historical periods.
