# financial-astrology

Financial astrology — planetary cycles and their correlation with markets, commodities, currencies, and economic trends. Covers planetary rulerships of commodities and sectors, Mercury retrograde and trading, eclipse impact on markets, Jupiter-Saturn cycles and economic phases, outer planet transits and secular trends, first-trade charts for stocks/crypto, lunar cycles and market sentiment, ingress timing, and the astrology of individual financial decisions. Use when analyzing market timing, commodity cycles, economic trends, or personal financial astrology.
