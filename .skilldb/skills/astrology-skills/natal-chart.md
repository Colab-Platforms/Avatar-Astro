# natal-chart

Cast and interpret a complete natal (birth) chart including planet placements, house positions, Ascendant/Rising sign, Sun sign, Moon sign, Big Three (Sun/Moon/ASC), Big Six (+ Mercury/Venus/Mars), dominant planets, dominant elements and modalities, and house system comparisons (Placidus, Whole Sign, Koch, Equal, Porphyry, Regiomontanus, Campanus, Alcabitius). Use when the user provides birth date, time, and location and wants a natal chart reading, birth chart interpretation, rising sign calculation, or dominant planet/element analysis.
