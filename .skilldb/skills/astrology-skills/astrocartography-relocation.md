# astrocartography-relocation

Astrocartography (astro mapping), relocation charts, and local space astrology. Maps planetary lines across the globe showing where each planet is angular (rising, setting, culminating, anti-culminating). Interpret how different locations activate different parts of the natal chart. Includes planetary line meanings, crossing points (parans), relocation chart casting, and practical relocation advice. Use when the user wants to know the best places to live, travel, or work based on their chart.
