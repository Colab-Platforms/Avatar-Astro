# birth-time-rectification

Birth time rectification techniques for determining or correcting an unknown or approximate birth time. Covers primary directions, solar arc directions to known events, progressed angles to events, transit correlation, Trutine of Hermes, prenatal epoch method, and reverse chart engineering (finding date/time/place from a chart image). Use when the user has an approximate birth time and wants to narrow it down, or when the birth time is unknown and needs to be estimated from life events.
