# Homepage Featured Image and Card Sizing Design

## Intent
Make the Live Darshan, Divya Margdarshak, and Devotion & Spirituality carousel imagery easier to see in the homepage's featured section.

## Approaches
- Enlarge both feature cards and carousel, preserving current content and responsive ordering.
- Enlarge only the photos and modestly increase the featured area's dimensions. Chosen to improve image clarity without a disproportionate change to the homepage layout.

## Scope and constraints
Keep Live Darshan and Divya Margdarshak side by side at desktop widths and stacked on mobile. Preserve all copy, links, thumbnails, carousel controls, and card styling. Increase card image heights from 160px to 176px at larger widths, and the carousel image width from 240px to 264px; add modest vertical room and widen the showcase container slightly.

## Acceptance
The images are visibly larger and legible; all three features remain in the same responsive section without clipping or overflow.
